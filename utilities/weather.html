Live Weather Lookup

Search any city and fetch current weather data from a public API.

Check Weather by City

Example: Hanoi, Da Nang, Tokyo, London

Type a city and press Get Weather.

How It Works

  1. Find city coordinates using Open-Meteo Geocoding API.
  2. Fetch current weather from Open-Meteo Forecast API.
  3. Render temperature, wind speed, and timestamp.
